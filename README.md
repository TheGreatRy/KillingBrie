# 🧀 Killing Brie  
*A First-Person Rodent Rampage by the Mouseketeers*

![Killing Brie Banner](banner.png) <!-- Optional: Replace with actual banner image if available -->

## 🎮 Overview

**Killing Brie** is a fast-paced first-person shooter where you play as a heavily armed commando mouse infiltrating a cat-infested bunker. Outnumbered and undersized, you'll have to fight claw and fang through hordes of feline enemies, teleport through strategic mouseholes, and vanish into makeshift hiding spots — all to bring the war for cheese to its explosive finale.

> "One mouse. Nine lives to take."

---

## 🚀 Features

- 🧠 **AI-Driven Cat Enemies**  
  Tactical cats that patrol, ambush, and fight back.

- 🕳️ **Teleporting Mouseholes**  
  Use a dynamic network of mouseholes to instantly move around the map — for surprise attacks or daring escapes.

- 🪑 **Prop-Based Stealth Mechanics**  
  Hide inside scattered props to outsmart enemies or avoid detection.

- 🔫 **Miniature Arsenal, Massive Mayhem**  
  Wield uniquely mouse-sized weapons engineered for maximum impact.

- 🛠️ **Powered by Unreal Engine 5**  
  Enjoy immersive lighting, responsive physics, and stunning visuals thanks to UE5.

## 📦 Installation

To install and play **Killing Brie**:

1. **Download the Latest Release**  
   Grab it from the [Releases](https://github.com/YOUR_USERNAME/killing-brie/releases) section.

2. **Extract the ZIP**  
   Unzip the folder to your preferred location.

3. **Run the Game**  
   Launch `KillingBrie.exe` (or the relevant executable for your platform).

4. **Optional**: Update GPU drivers for best performance.
